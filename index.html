FPV Configurator

A stripped-down Betaflight configurator that runs in the browser. Most of Betaflight's options are left out; what remains is what most pilots actually change. Connect the flight controller over USB in Chrome or Edge.

  • PID tuning with three sliders: damping, pitch gains and master multiplier
  • Filters cut down to gyro lowpass, RPM filter, dynamic notch, D-term and yaw lowpass
  • One rate profile, synced across axes, with a curve graph
  • Four modes: arm, angle, turtle and beeper
  • Ports assigned by device: pick the receiver, VTX or GPS, then its UART
  • Pre-flight checklist: bidirectional DShot, accelerometer, arm angle, beepers, airmode
  • Bluejay and AM32 ESC settings through the flight controller
  • OSD with a short list of elements and one profile
  • Analog VTX tables from presets

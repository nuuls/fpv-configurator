FPV Configurator

A simplified Betaflight configurator with none of the nerd shit.

Simplified setup

Only the settings most pilots actually change. PID tuning, for example, is three sliders.

Finds config issues

Checks the settings a quad should not fly without, like bidirectional DShot and a calibrated accelerometer, and offers a fix.

ESC configuration

Change Bluejay and AM32 ESC settings through the flight controller, without a separate app.

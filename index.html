FPV Configurator

A stripped-down Betaflight configurator that runs in the browser.

Simplified setup

Only the settings most pilots change. PID tuning is three sliders, there are four modes and one OSD profile, and ports are assigned by device instead of by UART.

Finds config issues

The Setup tab checks bidirectional DShot, accelerometer calibration, arm angle, RX-loss beepers and airmode, with a Fix button where it can. Settings changed elsewhere are listed with a reset to default.

ESC configuration

Read and change Bluejay 0.21 and AM32 2.21 ESC settings through the flight controller, without a separate ESC configurator.
